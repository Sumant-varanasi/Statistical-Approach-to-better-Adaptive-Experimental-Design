Headline

What the benchmark shows

Convergence

How fast each method closes the gap to the true optimum

Regret is how far the recommended operating point is from the true best, scaled so 0 is the optimum and 1 is a typical random operating point. Line is the median over seeds, band is the middle 50 %. Lower is better; the axis is logarithmic.

Results table

Final results after 50 experiments

Sorted best first. "Hit 1 %" is the share of seeds that got within 1 % of the optimum within the budget; "Experiments to 1 %" is the median number needed (a dash means most seeds never got there).

Statistics

Is the upgraded BO significantly better?

Every method is compared with GP + EI on final regret. BO variants share the same starting experiments per seed, so they use a paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test; the rest use a Mann-Whitney U test. P-values are Holm-corrected within each setting. A12 is the probability that a GP + EI run ends with lower regret than a run of the other method (0.5 = no difference).

Surrogate models

GP vs neural-network ensemble as a model of the twin

Same training data for every model, scored on 1,000 random test points and on 500 points near the optimum. NRMSE is prediction error divided by the spread of the test values (0 = perfect, 1 = useless). Coverage is how often the true value falls inside the model's 95 % interval; 0.95 is ideal, lower means over-confident.

How it works

Bayesian optimisation, one step at a time

One knob (temperature), objective = yield. Left: the GP's best guess and its uncertainty band after each experiment. Right: the acquisition function that picks the next experiment. EI targets the peak once the GP is confident about where it is.
